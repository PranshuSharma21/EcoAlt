The environmental footprint and long-term cost remain completely invisible.🌐 Our SolutionThink of EcoAlt as an intelligent, real-time decision co-pilot embedded into the quick-commerce experience.EcoAlt follows a strict Dual-Benefit Filter: it never guilt-trips users, and it only nudges them toward an alternative when switching satisfies both criteria:💰 The Customer Wins: Lower upfront cost (e.g. concentrated refills) or significant long-term savings over the product lifespan (e.g. washable bamboo cloths, rechargeable USB-C cells).🌱 The Planet Wins: Single-use plastic diverted from landfills, reduced secondary packaging, and lower transport emissions.If an item is already optimal (like recyclable milk pouches vs. heavy glass bottles), EcoAlt respects it and pushes zero artificial swaps.🏗️ Technical ArchitectureLayerTechnologies UsedDescriptionClient Hook & InterceptVanilla JavaScript, DOM ObserversIntercepts product SKU metadata, material composition tags, and
